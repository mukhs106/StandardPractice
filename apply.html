An application for a Permit must be made in the prescribed form (Form P-1) and must describe the Work, its defined outcome, the Applicant’s Standing, and their Sponsor (5.1).

The Work

Outcome

Work without a defined outcome is classified as Personal Activity (3.4). You may continue.

Your Standing

Grounds for Standing (Schedule A)

Work that demonstrably changed an outcome for a client or institution

Verified by: The client

Selection, publication, or membership by a body of Standing

Verified by: The body

Education, employment or commission from institutions of Standing

Verified by: The institution

Command of the formal rules of the medium

Verified by: — (see 4.3)

Craft alone shall not constitute Standing (4.3).

Teaching, speaking, publishing or mentoring

Verified by: The audience

Recognised Practice is counted from the date of your first Permit (1.10).

Your Sponsor

A Sponsor must be a Practitioner of Standing (1.11). Applications without a Sponsor are received (5.5).

The declaration must be confirmed before your application can be received.
